## 1. Physics striker: 66.0% -> 97.1% on the test shots (done)

| Case (test seed 30000) | Old striker (`strike_planner.py`) | New striker (`sim_striker.py`) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 block | 76.0% | **99.5%** |
| 2 blocks | 69.5% | **97.0%** |
| 3 blocks | 60.0% | **96.0%** |
| Block on the straight line | 58.5% | **96.0%** |
| Mean | 66.0% | **97.1%** |

Validation: 98.8% (seed 50000) and 98.0% (seed 60000). Files: `results/2026-11-02/physics_striker/`.

What was wrong and what changed:
- **Scoring bug (fixed for all rules):** `_crossed_mouth`, which catches fast diagonal goals crossing the goal line
  between 20 Hz samples, ran only with `obstacle_bounce`. Under the clean rules such goals were scored "escaped".
  All 41 "escaped" shots of AB-seed3-bank's 1-block baseline were real goals, so its 1-block score under the clean
  rules is about 70%, not 49%. Test: `test_fast_diagonal_goal_counts_without_obstacle_bounce`.
- **Execution, not shot choice, was the problem.** Clear lines to the goal tolerate only about +-3 degrees; the
  closed-form strike solve missed by a median 3.9 degrees on failed shots (paddle 0.26 m/s faster than planned,
  puck 0.58 m/s faster). `scripts/sim_striker.py` (`SimStrikePlanner`) copies the observed state into a private
  simulator just before striking, plays the strike there, measures the puck's real heading, rotates its aim by
  the error and repeats (3 iterations), and falls back to up to 8 other plans if the copy does not score.
  Only observations are used; table friction is estimated from how fast the puck slows.
- **Relaxed fallback planning:** for pucks along the side wall every normal plan was rejected (run-up point outside
  the paddle area, or too little time). When there is no plan at all it now retries with shorter run-ups and
  softer shots; those are test-fired in the copy too.
- Use it: `python -m scripts.eval_suite --controller --set sim=1`. About 0.13 s of compute per shot.

## 2-4. Imitation vs imitation + PPO

`scripts/train_dagger.py --clean --sim-teacher`: teacher = the new striker (96-97% even with randomised friction).
Iteration 0 = pure copying (behaviour cloning); later iterations = DAgger. Every iteration is scored on the strict
validation suite (`validation_by_condition` in the manifest history).

### 2-hour TACC study (job 3547783, finished; raw files in `$WORK/airhockey-runs/imitation-dev`)

Validation mean of the 4 cases (iterations: 100 shots per case; "best DAgger": 200 per case):

| Group | Pure copying | Best DAgger | After PPO, old settings (3M steps, 3 seeds) |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1: 1M labelled steps/iter, 256 net | 10.2% | 33.8% | 12-16% |
| D2: 1M/iter, 512 net | 15.2% | 36.0% | 8-10% |
| D3: 2M/iter, 512 net | 20.0% | **42.9%** (68% with 1 block) | 9-13% |
| D4: 1M/iter, 256 net, 50% blocked line | 16.5% | 31.2% | 13-15% |

Local run (5 iterations x 250k steps, `results/2026-11-02/imitation_local/`): 6% -> 23.5%. PPO probe numbers: `results/2026-11-02/ppo_fix_local/probe_results.json`.

Findings:
- **Imitation improves with data but is far from 90% at 5-10M labelled steps.** Action error is small
  (MSE ~0.01) but the task needs about +-1 degree of aim. The teacher also depends on things the network cannot
  see (its committed plan and the aim correction it found in its simulator copy), so identical-looking states get
  slightly different labels.
- **PPO with default fine-tuning settings destroyed the imitation skill.** Cause: exploration noise. With log std
  -1.6 (about 0.4 m/s of noise) the 40.5% imitation network scores 13%; PPO learns from that broken behaviour and
  its untrained value network pushes the policy the wrong way (22% after 0.5M steps).
- **Fix** (`train_precision_striker.py`): `--resume-log-std -3.5 --resume-lr 3e-5 --target-kl 0.02
  --critic-warmup 1000000 --freeze-norm`. Local check from the D3 network (200 shots): 40.5% -> 45.0% at 0.6M,
  42.5% at 0.8M. No collapse; a gain is not proven yet (+-3.5 points noise).
